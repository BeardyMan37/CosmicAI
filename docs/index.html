Regression Scan Statistics for ALMA Platforming

An interpretable interval-anomaly detector for ALMA QA2 calibration spectra. The method searches for compact platforming artifacts while preserving smooth, non-flat bandpass structure.

The astronomy problem

Localized calibration artifacts can propagate into science products.

ALMA bandpass calibration spectra are an early checkpoint before science-ready hyperspectral cubes are built. Platforming appears as a compact amplitude offset over contiguous frequency channels. It can look small in one row, but downstream it can introduce non-astrophysical structure.

A useful detector should identify the affected row, localize the implicated frequency interval, and provide a smooth fitted background for auditability.

Method

Scan intervals, fit smooth backgrounds, return an auditable score.

1

Search candidate intervals

Platforming is treated as a localized interval anomaly rather than a point anomaly or two unrelated changepoints.

2

Fit inside and outside

The score compares one global smooth fit against separate fits for the candidate interval and the remaining channels.

3

Use NWKR for local smoothness

Nadaraya-Watson kernel regression follows broad bandpass curvature without forcing a single global polynomial.

4

Update efficiently

Cached scan state and local kernel support turn brute-force rescoring into efficient streaming-style updates.

Regression scan statistic

S(Ia,b) = 1 - (SSEin(Ia,b) + SSEout(Ia,b)) / SSEall

A large score means one compact interval explains the spectrum much better when separated from the smooth background. The detector reports the maximum-scoring interval and its score.

What the detector returns

  • Suspicion score for each spectrum
  • Frequency interval causing the flag
  • Fitted smooth background
  • Review-ready evidence for QA workflows

Why it matters

  • Platforming is rare but scientifically consequential
  • False negatives can corrupt downstream data products
  • Interval localization supports human inspection
  • The method is designed for scalable QA2 screening
